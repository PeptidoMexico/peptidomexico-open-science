# Code of Conduct

We want the open-source package community to be useful, rigorous and welcoming.

Participants are expected to communicate professionally, assume good faith,
explain technical disagreements with evidence and make room for different levels
of scientific and software experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
personal attacks, deliberate misinformation about safety or research results,
spam, astroturfing and attempts to manipulate ratings, citations or links.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect
the project and its contributors. Report a concern privately through the address in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), or use the repository's private reporting mechanism
once the public canonical repository is available.
